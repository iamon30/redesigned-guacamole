Rules, Consent & Competition

  • Attempts: maximum of 3 attempts per topic. An attempt is counted the moment you press Start — refreshing or closing the page does not give it back.
  • Tab lock: leaving this tab or window (switching tabs, apps, split screen) closes the quiz, voids it with a score of 0 and uses an attempt.
  • Timer: one item at a time, 40 s (concept items), 75 s (calculation items). Time runs out → the next item opens automatically; unanswered items score 0. No going back.
  • Privacy picture: each question is shown as a blurred, distorted picture; only a thin strip under your mouse or finger is readable, and you answer with the A–D buttons. This is normal — it protects the quiz against AI and camera tools (Google Lens, Gemini, photos).
  • Unique items: your 5 questions and numbers are generated for you alone — answers from classmates will not match.
  • Honesty: no screenshots, no sharing questions, no AI tools or other people helping. Violations may be disqualified from the ranking and prizes.
  • Records: your name, section, email, start/end timestamps, score and answer key are saved to the instructor's Google Sheet. A result picture downloads automatically after every quiz — keep it as proof.
  • Ranking (optional): your total = sum of your best score in each topic (max 45). Section rank = average of the top 5 totals in the section (at least 3 joined students needed). Ties: higher total, then fewer attempts. Only students who join the ranking appear on the leaderboard (name, section, total) and are eligible for prizes and the section ranking.
  • Prizes: Top Section ₱500 · Top 10 students (any section): 1st ₱150 · 2nd ₱100 · 3rd ₱50 · 4th–10th ₱20 each. The instructor's decision is final; the Google Sheet is the official record. One account per email.
